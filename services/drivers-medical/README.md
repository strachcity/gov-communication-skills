# DVLA Drivers Medical

Service-specific knowledge for DVLA Drivers Medical communications.

Drivers Medical handles cases where a driver's medical condition may affect whether they can hold a driving licence. Many customers are waiting to find out if they can still drive.

## What belongs here

- Drivers Medical vocabulary, and internal terms to keep out of customer communications
- policy decisions about Drivers Medical communications, with their owner and date
- which channels Drivers Medical uses, and what each one can carry
- open policy and legal questions, recorded as questions until someone with authority answers them

## What does not belong here

- anything that would be true for another DVLA service or another department, which goes in a generic skill
