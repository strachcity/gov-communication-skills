# Privacy knowledge

Privacy-aware communication principles under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, for any government service.

This is not legal advice. Entries record what a source says and what has been confirmed. Anything that changes a real communication needs confirming by the service's data protection officer or legal team.

## What belongs here

- what personal data can and cannot go in each channel, like letter, email, text message, phone, web account and notification
- how special category data, including health data, affects what a communication can say and through which channel
- verifying identity before sharing information
- third parties, like a GP, a carer or someone acting on the customer's behalf
- the sources these come from, like the legislation and ICO guidance

## What does not belong here

- a single service's decisions about its own channels or data, which go in `services/`
- wording and style, which goes in `knowledge/content-design/`

## Unknown positions

If the legal position is not clear, record it as an open question. Do not infer it from what services usually do.
