# Content design knowledge

GOV.UK content design and writing guidance that applies to any government service.

## What belongs here

- style rules from the GOV.UK A to Z style guide
- writing guidelines on user needs, structure, plain English, tone, links, titles and summaries
- accessibility and inclusive language guidance that affects wording
- the order to check sources in when guidance is unclear

Primary source: https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/

## What does not belong here

- anything about a single service's vocabulary, letters, decisions or timescales, which goes in `services/`
- anything about what personal data a communication can contain, which goes in `knowledge/privacy/`
- instructions for how to run a review or draft, which go in `skills/`
