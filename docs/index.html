Draft and review case communications for any government service

Agent skills for Claude and Microsoft 365 Copilot. They adapt generic message patterns using your service's facts, check GOV.UK style and privacy, and list anything your service still needs to decide.

Install See an example

How it works

  1. Tell it about your service, or point it to your service's documentation.
  2. It creates a service context with you, or reads the one you already have. You keep it in your own files.
  3. It finds the pattern that fits your request and adapts it with your service's confirmed facts.
  4. It checks what each version reveals in each channel, and applies GOV.UK content guidance.
  5. It returns a draft for each channel and a list of what still needs a decision from your service.

It doesn't invent your policy. Anything your service hasn't decided, like a timescale or what a message can disclose, is flagged for a person to decide.

The 9 moments

Each moment has a pattern, with baseline copy for email, text message, letter and status page. Not every service uses every moment, and some happen more than once.

Later

9You need to act before a date

Reminder, final reminder and delay change how a moment is said. Asking for feedback can follow a moment, and never implies it affects the case.

What happens next
We'll update you by 18 October 2026, or sooner if we get what we need.

  • No invented timescaleThe update date comes from a confirmed commitment, not a guess at when the third party replies
  • No unapproved disclosureNeither version describes the third party. The text follows a confirmed decision
  • GOV.UK content guidance appliedWhat happens next and when, and "You do not need to do anything now"
Needs a service decision

Can the email say we're waiting on "the local council"?

It's recorded as a hypothesis, not a decision. The data protection officer decides. Until then, the email says "someone else".

What each message reveals

For a set of messages, the privacy skill lists what each one reveals in each channel. Reviewers check the flagged rows, and their answers go into the service context as confirmed decisions.
