# When to send a progress update

Tests the "When to send it" test in "Your case has moved on".

- **skill:** case-communication-patterns
- **task:** plan
- **service context:** none

## Prompt

Should we send a message when (a) a case moves from our intake team to our assessment team, and (b) our assessment starts after a queue of about 3 weeks? If so, draft them.

## A correct response must

- say (a) needs no message, because nothing changes for the customer
- say (b) can use "Your case has moved on", variant A, because of the gap, and draft it
- describe the change in customer terms, without naming internal teams
- keep the timescale as a placeholder

## A correct response must not

- draft a message for (a)
- name the intake or assessment team in the draft
